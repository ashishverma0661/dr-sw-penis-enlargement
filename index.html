FAQ

Things men ask before they book.

What makes penis enlargement at Dr SW different?
Dr. Sherif Wakil has performed regenerative and aesthetic men's intimate procedures for over a decade. The technique, the precision and the clinical environment are all consultant-led, not delegated.
Is the treatment safe?
Hyaluronic acid is one of the most studied dermal fillers in modern medicine and is naturally found in the human body. The procedure is performed in a CQC-registered private clinic by a GMC-registered doctor. Mild bruising or tenderness is the most common side effect.
What is the recovery time?
Most patients return to normal activity within 24 hours, including work and gentle exercise. Sexual activity is typically advised 2 weeks after the procedure to allow the filler to settle.
How long does the result last?
A single session typically lasts 12 to 18 months. Many patients top up annually rather than start over. The filler is gradually broken down by the body, the same as any HA filler. We will not guarantee a specific clinical outcome — we will tell you, honestly, what realistic results look like for you.
Is the consultation confidential?
Yes. Dr SW Clinics operates dedicated consultation suites and full medical confidentiality. Nothing is shared outside your treating doctor.
Where is the clinic?
Central London (Harley Street) and Dubai. Both are private clinics with full medical infrastructure and dedicated treatment rooms.
